`Tag` takes a leading `icon` (a Lucide icon, small and decorative, spaced by the same gap as Button) and a controlled `copied` state with `copiedLabel` (default "Copied") that swaps the label for a Check icon and announces it through a polite live region. New `useTransientFlag` hook turns a click into a short-lived `copied`.
