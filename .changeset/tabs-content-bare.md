`TabsContent` takes `variant="bare"` for tabs that are an app's top-level nav or a page's sections: no tinted fill, no padding, no radius and no pull-up under the strip, so the panel sits on the page. The default `variant="panel"` is unchanged (hds#819). Replaces the inline style job-hunt put on every top-level panel.
