New `MetaLine` primitive: one or two items joined by a bullet (a third is a type error). New `hds/max-two-meta-items` ESLint rule, in `hds.configs.recommended`, flags `A · B · C` chains. Max two items per bullet line, so translations never wrap mid-line; more facts get their own row.
