HDS separates things by **tone and space**, not by hairline outlines. A card, a panel, a
popover or a list sits on its own surface step; controls are soft filled and carry a
zero-blur **hard shadow** from one light source (straight above), in the control's own colour. Borders are kept only where they
carry meaning.

## The surface steps

| Step                | Light       | Dark        | Use                                                             |
| ------------------- | ----------- | ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `surface.page`      | white       | black       | The page.                                                       |
| `surface.raised`    | neutral 50  | neutral 850 | Cards, panels, code blocks, prose tables.                       |
| `surface.muted`     | neutral 100 | neutral 800 | Tracks, tags, selected chips, canvases a raised card sits on.   |
| `surface.overlay`   | neutral 100 | neutral 800 | Popover, Menu, Select, Dialog, Toast (plus the overlay shadow). |
| `surface.soft`      | neutral 200 | neutral 700 | The soft control fill: secondary Button.                        |
| `surface.softHover` | neutral 300 | neutral 600 | Hovered soft control (the wipe colour).                         |
| `surface.sunken`    | neutral 200 | neutral 700 | Inset wells and the highlighted menu item.                      |

## Where borders stay

- **Inputs**: Input, Textarea, Select and Combobox triggers, TokenField, Checkbox, Radio, Switch.
- **Focus rings** and **selected or active states** (a selected Tag, a selected SegmentedControl segment, the active Tab underline, a selectable Card).
- **Feedback tones**: a `tone` Card or Button, where the border is the signal; Card `variant="accent"`.
- **Dense data**: the Table frame (a full 1px `border.default` outline, hds#1198) and its row and
  column dividers; Divider; Timeline connectors.
- **Kbd**: the key edge.

Everything else (Card, Dialog, Menu, Popover, Toast, CodeBlock, the Tabs list,
the SegmentedControl track, Tag pills and chips) has no resting outline. To bring one back on a
Card, pass `bordered`.

## Buttons: soft fill, hard shadow, wipe

- Secondary is a soft tonal fill (`surface.soft`), no border.
- Primary and secondary carry the hard shadow on a **fixed base**: the bottom edge never
  moves — 3px at rest, 4px on hover (the button lifts 1px), 1px on press (it sinks 2px).
  Tertiary is the ghost: no edge, no movement, a `surface.hover` fill on hover, deeper on
  press. Kbd carries a 2px edge.
- The edge is the item's **own fill, stepped darker** (chroma ×0.7225, same hue): primary from
  the accent, secondary from the soft fill, a tone from its tone fill, Kbd from its key fill. In
  light it drops two OKLCH steps (lightness −0.12); in dark a third of the way to the card. The
  values ship as `--hds-edge-*`; set `--hds-btn-edge` or `--hds-kbd-edge` to override one.
- Selected states (a pressed toggle, the selected segment, a selected filter Tag) take the
  primary fill and the primary edge.
- The hover fill wipes in from the edge the pointer enters.
- Opt out per button: `shadow="none"`, `hoverEffect="none"`.

## Contrast

- In both themes every edge is darker than its fill. `tests/raised-edge.test.ts` recomputes each
  edge from its fill.
- The own-colour edge is a depth cue, not a 3:1 boundary: a soft secondary is identified by its
  label and fill. For a 3:1 edge, set `--hds-btn-edge: var(--semantic-color-shadow-hard)`.
- Text on every soft fill clears AA (4.5:1). `tests/soft-surfaces-contrast.test.ts` checks it.

<PrinciplesAndPractices>

### Principle

- **Tone and space separate.** A surface step and whitespace do what a hairline used to.
- **A border is a signal.** Keep it for inputs, focus, selection, feedback and dense data.
- **One light source.** Every hard shadow drops straight down, in its own colour stepped darker.

### Best practices

- Put grouped content on `surface.raised`; put a canvas behind raised cards on `surface.muted`.
- Use the secondary Button for most actions; it is the soft one.
- Don't add `border` to a Card to group it; use space or a surface step.
- Don't turn off the hard shadow on one button in a group and not the others.

</PrinciplesAndPractices>
