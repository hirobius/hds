Motion in Hirobius provides physical feedback and spatial clarity without introducing unnecessary visual noise.

## Principle

Animation serves functional and interaction affordances across UI states:

- **Productive Motion**: Fast, direct transitions (150ms) designed for micro-interactions, hover states, and focus changes.
- **Expressive Motion**: Physics-aware movement (250ms) reserved for significant UI entrances and teaching moments.
- **Spatial Motion**: Controlled movement (400ms) for elements traveling larger distances across the viewport.

## Best practices

- Use productive motion tokens for interactive feedback on hover, focus, and press states.
- Respect reduced-motion user preferences and accessibility settings.
- Keep transition durations short to keep application flows feeling responsive.
- Don't apply playful or bouncy springs to routine interactive controls.
- Don't use expressive or spatial motion for simple state toggles or micro-interactions.
- Don't hardcode CSS transition durations or cubic-bezier curves outside system tokens.
