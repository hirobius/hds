## Principle

- **4px grid.** Every step on the scale is a multiple of 4px. Skip half steps and one-off values.
- **Space, not lines.** Separate sections with whitespace before reaching for borders.
- **Density.** Compact mode tightens the whole scale at once, so layouts keep their proportions.

## Best practices

- Lay out pages with `Stack`, `Grid` and `Box` and the spacing tokens.
- Give containers and cards the same inner padding.
- Pick a larger step for a bigger break between groups, a smaller one inside a group.
- Don't write pixel values or half steps in margins or padding.
- Don't squeeze whitespace to fit more on one screen.
- Don't mix gap sizes inside one stack or grid.
