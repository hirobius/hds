## Density

The default rhythm is roomy, for sites. Dense apps opt into a tighter one by putting `data-density="compact"` on `<html>` or any ancestor (in React, `HdsThemeProvider density="compact"`). The values come from the tokens' Compact mode, so components follow without a prop. Try it with **Density** in the display settings, the gear beside the theme switcher.

| Role (px)               | Default           | Compact           |
| ----------------------- | ----------------- | ----------------- |
| Scale sm · md · lg · xl | 20 · 28 · 40 · 64 | 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 |
| Scale 2xs · xs          | 4 · 8             | 4 · 8             |
| Section stack           | 96                | 48                |
| Body · ui · caption     | 18 · 16 · 14      | 15 · 14 · 13      |
| Title                   | 28                | 24                |
| Display                 | 56                | 56                |

### Principle

- **4px grid.** Every step on the scale is a multiple of 4px. Skip half steps and one-off values.
- **Space, not lines.** Separate sections with whitespace before reaching for borders.

### Best practices

- Lay out pages with `Stack`, `Grid` and `Box` and the spacing tokens.
- Give containers and cards the same inner padding.
- Pick a larger step for a bigger break between groups, a smaller one inside a group.
- Don't write pixel values or half steps in margins or padding.
- Don't squeeze whitespace to fit more on one screen.
- Don't mix gap sizes inside one stack or grid.

</PrinciplesAndPractices>
