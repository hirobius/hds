Spacing in Hirobius creates deliberate, breathable, and predictable layouts across screen densities and viewports.

## Principle

The spacing architecture operates as a structural material across all layouts:

- **4px Base Unit**: Every spacing step snaps to the primitive scale based on a 4px grid; half-steps and arbitrary pixel values are avoided.
- **Whitespace as Structure**: Generous, intentional spacing separates content sections without relying on heavy borders or decorative lines.
- **Density Adaptation**: Spacing tokens respond systematically to density modes (such as compact mode) to maintain proportional visual rhythm.

## Best practices

- Use spacing tokens alongside layout components such as `Stack`, `Grid`, and `Box` to structure pages.
- Maintain consistent internal padding across containers and card surfaces.
- Choose spacing tokens based on structural hierarchy and content relationship.
- Don't hardcode arbitrary pixel values or half-step increments in margins or padding.
- Don't compress layout whitespace to fit excess content onto a single screen.
- Don't mix inconsistent gap values within a single stack or grid section.
