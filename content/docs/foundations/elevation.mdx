## Principle

- **Flat first.** Most content sits on the page with a hairline border, not a shadow.
- **Height means layer.** Raised for cards, floating for menus and popovers, overlay for dialogs.
- **Soft in dark.** Shadows use one shadow color that turns black in dark mode.

## Best practices

- Pick the level by what the element is, not how much it should stand out.
- Use one level per layer of the page.
- Don't stack shadows or write your own `box-shadow`.
- Don't raise static content to draw attention.
