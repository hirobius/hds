## Who it's for

Teams building Hirobius products, and the agents that build alongside them.

## Voice

- Use plain words a reader already knows. Name a thing by what it does, not by how it is built.
- Keep sentences short, one idea each.
- Tell the reader what to do. Leave out brand adjectives.
- Keep maintainer references off public pages: issue and ADR numbers, script names, internal counts.

## Words we don't use

- systems-first
