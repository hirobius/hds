Making HDS more delightful

Three directions, from subtle to bold. Each one renders the same set: the docs Foundations header, a button set, a card, a Companies table row and the Roleforge Companies screen at 390px, in light and dark. Every specimen reads HDS's real token names; a direction only overrides values, scoped to this page.

MetalBear's site is the mood reference: big confident type, a warm two-colour accent, chunky cards with hard shadows, pill kickers and open space. None of their colours, typeface or assets are used. A dashed square marks where the new hydra logo goes.

Recommendation

Ship direction 2 as the system, keep direction 3 as a tenant

  1. Adopt direction 2 (Expressive type) in the core. The display face and pill kickers carry most of the personality MetalBear has, while touching only the display and title steps, the accent ramp and one new kicker role. Product screens like Roleforge stay calm because body, UI and table text do not change.
  2. Take two pieces of direction 1 with it: the rounder radius (one knob, role.radius) and the soft offset shadow on interactive cards only. Both fit the current elevation model.
  3. Make direction 3 a demo tenant, not the default. It is the most fun, and all its pairs pass AA, but it breaks five written rules in DESIGN.md (tinted surfaces, border plus shadow, transform on press, bouncy motion, two accents). That is exactly what a tenant overlay is for, like brutalist-demo; the hydra logo could debut there.
  4. Before any token change: an ADR for the second accent and display face, the DESIGN.md rules it amends, and the hydra logo in the slot to check the palette against it.
